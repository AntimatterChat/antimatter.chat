Antimatter vs Element (Matrix)

Element is the best-known app of Matrix, an open, federated protocol for secure chat. Its server can be self-hosted, with a paid suite for larger organisations.

Why teams choose Antimatter

  • Built for teams and communities alike: threads, voice channels, roles and badges, onboarding and moderation tools.
  • Apps built in: notes, whiteboards, boards, playbooks, polls and todo lists, plus AI and call transcripts with your own models.
  • Every feature included, with no paid server edition.

When Element (Matrix) may suit you better

  • You need end-to-end encryption by default, everywhere.
  • You want to federate with the open Matrix network, or bridge to other chat networks.
  • You work with organisations that have standardised on Matrix.
