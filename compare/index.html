One app at a time

Each comparison also says when the other app may suit you better.

Antimatter vs Slack

Slack is the best-known team chat. It runs only in Slack's cloud, and much of it is behind paid plans.

Antimatter vs Mattermost

Antimatter is a fork of Mattermost: it keeps its server, its APIs and its integrations. What differs is the licensing, the interface and the apps.

Antimatter vs Rocket.Chat

Rocket.Chat is another open-source, self-hostable chat, with a focus on omnichannel customer service and federation.

Antimatter vs Discord

Discord popularised voice channels and community servers. It is built for communities and gaming, and runs only in Discord's cloud.

Antimatter vs Microsoft Teams

Microsoft Teams comes with Microsoft 365. It is deeply tied to Microsoft's cloud and licensing.

Antimatter vs Element (Matrix)

Element is the best-known app of Matrix, an open, federated protocol for secure chat. Its server can be self-hosted, with a paid suite for larger organisations.

Antimatter

Self-hosted, open-source team chat: channels, threads, calls and voice rooms, apps and AI, on your own server.
