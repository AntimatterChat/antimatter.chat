Features

Everything your team needs, in one place you own

A tour of Antimatter, with screenshots taken from a demo server. Every feature on this page is included in the free software, with nothing held back for a paid edition.

Messaging

Conversations that stay organised

Channels grouped in categories, direct and group messages, and threads, in an interface built for long working days.

  • Channels and categories: public and private channels, categories set by admins for everyone, favorites and following.
  • Threads and named sub-chats: reply in threads, give a thread a name to list it under its channel, follow the ones you care about.
  • Rich messages: Markdown, code with syntax highlighting, tables, LaTeX, link previews, files with previews.
  • Message priority: urgent and important messages, persistent notifications and acknowledgements.
  • Scheduled messages and drafts, reminders, forwarding, editing with history, and saved messages.
  • Search across messages and files, with filters, exact phrases and dates.
Communities

Communities with roles, badges and their own rules

Run a company or a community: Antimatter has the tools to welcome people and keep the place pleasant.

  • Display roles and team badges: coloured roles, roles people can pick themselves, badges next to names.
  • Onboarding: a welcome screen and questions that give newcomers the right roles and channels.
  • Permission overrides per category and channel, on top of the server's permission schemes.
  • Moderation: timeouts, a moderator view, message reporting and quarantine.
Apps

Collaborative apps, built in

The apps open beside the chat, in a panel or a window of their own, and anything you share with a channel updates live for everyone in it.

Notes

Documents edited together in real time, with headings, lists, checklists and code. Keep them for yourself, or share them with a channel.

Whiteboards

A shared canvas with shapes, arrows, text, shape libraries your admins add, and everyone's cursors, without calling any outside service.

Kanban

Boards, tables and calendars for projects, with cards, properties and views, linked to the channels that work on them.

Playbooks

Checklists for incidents, releases and onboarding, with owners, status updates and retrospectives.

Todo lists

Personal and shared tasks, assigned from any message.

Calendar

Your CalDAV calendar beside the chat, with reminders and call links.

Mail

Your mailbox, over IMAP and SMTP, in the app.

Polls

Single or multiple choice, with an end time and live results.

AI

AI that runs where you decide

Help when you ask for it, and quiet the rest of the time. Antimatter's agents work with OpenAI, Anthropic, Azure, Mistral, Gemini, Bedrock, or any OpenAI-compatible server you run yourself, such as vLLM, llama.cpp or Ollama.

Rewrite, fix and translate as you write

Ask the composer to fix spelling and grammar, shorten, clarify, change the tone of a message or translate it before it's sent.

The agent panel

Chat with your agents next to any channel: summarize it, find a decision, draft a reply, or use tools through MCP servers.

Call transcripts and summaries

Calls are transcribed by your speech-to-text model, shown as live captions, then posted as a transcript with an AI summary in the call's thread.

Recaps and channel summaries

Catch up after time off with recaps of the channels you choose, and summaries of long threads.

Automatic translation

Channels translated into each reader's language, with LibreTranslate or any OpenAI-compatible model.

Read aloud

Any message read out by your text-to-speech model.

Smart replies

Agents can answer in channels you choose, with your team's permissions.

Calls and voice channels

Voice rooms that are always open

Voice channels sit in the sidebar like any other channel: click to join, see who's there, and talk. Calls start from any conversation too.

  • Video, screen sharing and background blur, with quality presets for sharing code or slides.
  • Raised hands with a queue, reactions and per-person volume.
  • Push-to-talk, host controls and a call window of its own.
  • Live captions and transcripts from your own speech-to-text server, with no extra infrastructure.
Integrations and ChatOps

Your tools, talking in your channels

A complete REST API and plugin system, with integrations of its own.

  • GitHub, GitLab and Jira subscriptions, previews and slash commands.
  • Incoming and outgoing webhooks, slash commands, bots and OAuth apps.
  • Interactive messages and dialogs: buttons, menus and forms that post back to your services.
  • Plugins that add apps, panels, commands and message types to Antimatter's interface.
Administration and security

Everything an administrator expects

A complete System Console, with every setting available on every server.

  • Authentication: SAML 2.0, OpenID Connect, Google, Microsoft Entra, LDAP/Active Directory, MFA.
  • Access control: permission schemes, attribute-based access, guest accounts, IP filtering.
  • Compliance: data retention, compliance and message export, audit logging.
  • Operations: Elasticsearch or OpenSearch search, Prometheus metrics, S3-compatible file storage.
  • Branding: your site name, logo and login text, custom emoji and themes.
The little details

The small things people notice

Times in your time zone

"3pm CET" in a message shows in each reader's own time.

"They may be asleep"

A note when it's the middle of the night for the person you're writing to, with a button to send it in their morning.

Autolinks

Each community defines patterns, like ticket keys, that become links as messages are posted.

Acknowledgements

Ask for an acknowledgement on important messages, and see who has acknowledged them.

Burn on read

Sensitive messages that disappear after they've been read.

Spoilers

Hidden until each reader chooses to reveal them.

Conversation inbox

Each conversation's mentions, saved and pinned messages, files and threads, one click away from its header; one more click shows the whole community's.

Team badges

Click the team badge next to a name to see the team, its lead and its members, and jump to its channel.

Put on top

Keep an important message above the conversation, for everyone, for an hour, a day, a week or until it's removed.

Reminders

Have a message come back to you later, from its menu.

Send in their morning

Schedule a message for the recipient's working hours.

Note to self

A personal space to forward messages to and keep notes.

Streamer mode

Hide personal information while sharing your screen.

38 languages

Including right-to-left layouts for Arabic, Hebrew, Persian and Urdu.

Your own GIFs and stickers

Collections hosted on your server, no tracking.

Themes

Dark, light and OLED themes, following your system, and shareable custom themes.

Memes

Pick one of sixty templates from the emoji picker, write the captions, and post the meme.

Private notes on people

Remember who's who, visible only to you.

Channel export

Download a channel's messages as a spreadsheet file, from its menu or with /export.

Named threads

Give a thread a name and it becomes a sub-chat of its channel, in its followers' sidebars.

Community folders

Gather your communities into folders in the rail.

Try it on your own server

Install Antimatter in a few minutes and invite your team.
