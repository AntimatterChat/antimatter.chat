Your team's chat, calls and apps, on your own server

Antimatter brings channels, threads, voice rooms, collaborative apps and AI together in one place you run yourself, with no paywall.

Why Antimatter

Your conversations stay yours

A chat server holds your team's decisions, files and history. Antimatter keeps them on infrastructure you control, with no vendor deciding which features you may use.

Self-hosted

Run it on your own hardware or cloud, with Docker or a release archive and PostgreSQL. It works fully offline, behind your firewall.

Mattermost's foundations

Built on the Mattermost server, used by large organisations for years: proven message delivery, permissions and scaling.

Enterprise features, no licence needed

No SSO tax: single sign-on with SAML and OpenID Connect, LDAP sync, data retention, compliance export and more come with every server, for every team.

Open source

The whole server is free software under the AGPL v3.0: read it, audit it, change it, and run it for as many people as you like.

Security and reliability

Built on a server that has carried critical work for a decade

Antimatter runs on a mature, open-source server trusted where security matters: dependable message delivery, a stable data model and a documented API, so your deployments and integrations keep working from one version to the next.

  • Your identity provider: SAML, OpenID Connect, Google, Microsoft Entra, LDAP and Active Directory sync, and multi-factor authentication.
  • Fine-grained access: roles, permission schemes, attribute-based access control, guest accounts and IP filtering.
  • Compliance: data retention policies, compliance export, audit logs, classification banners and burn-on-read messages.
  • No telemetry: the server sends nothing home. Outside connections only happen for the features you turn on.
Apps and ChatOps

Work together without leaving the conversation

Antimatter's apps open next to the chat, share with a channel in one click, and update live for everyone.

  • Notes: collaborative documents, edited together in real time.
  • Whiteboards: draw, sketch diagrams and brainstorm on a shared canvas.
  • Boards and Playbooks: kanban boards for projects, checklists for incidents and repeatable processes.
  • Todo lists, polls, calendar and mail: tasks, quick decisions, your events and your inbox, beside your team.
AI, on your terms

Assistants that work with the models you choose

AI stays in its corner: a button in the composer, a panel you open, a summary when you ask for one. It's there when you need a hand, and out of the way the rest of the time. Connect any OpenAI-compatible model, including ones running on your own GPUs, and your data goes nowhere you didn't decide.

  • Rewrite and fix a message before sending: clearer, shorter, friendlier, or simply correct.
  • Call transcripts and summaries: live captions during calls, then the transcript and a summary in the call's thread.
  • The agent panel: ask questions about a channel, a thread or your work, with your permissions.
  • Channel summaries and recaps: catch up on what you missed in a few lines.
  • Automatic translation of channels, and any message read out loud on request.
Calls and voice rooms

Talk as easily as you type

Start a call from any conversation, or drop into a voice channel that is always open, the way your team would walk into a meeting room.

  • Voice channels in the sidebar, showing who's there and who's speaking.
  • Video and screen sharing with quality presets, background blur and picture-in-picture.
  • Raised hands, reactions and per-person volume, push-to-talk and host controls.
  • Live captions and transcripts with your own speech-to-text model.
Integrations

Connected to the tools you already use

A complete REST API, webhooks, bots and plugins bring your other tools into the conversation, and integrations written for Mattermost work too.

  • GitHub, GitLab and Jira: notifications, previews and actions in your channels.
  • Webhooks, slash commands and bots to connect anything, from CI pipelines to monitoring.
  • Interactive messages and dialogs for approvals and forms right in the chat.
  • Plugins that add apps, message types, commands and panels.

Get started

Up and running in minutes

One compose file brings up Antimatter with its database, its file storage and its GIF service. The first account you create becomes the administrator.

$ curl -fsSLO https://antimatter.chat/compose.yaml
$ cat > .env <<EOF
SITE_URL=http://localhost:8065
POSTGRES_PASSWORD=$(openssl rand -hex 24)
S3_SECRET_KEY=$(openssl rand -hex 24)
GIFS_API_KEY=$(openssl rand -hex 24)
EOF
$ docker compose up -d
