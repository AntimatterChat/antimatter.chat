About agents

An agent is an AI assistant that appears in Antimatter as a bot, with its own name. Your administrator connects agents to an AI service, which may run on your organisation's own servers or at an outside provider, and decides who may use each agent and in which channels. If your server has no agents, or none you may use, the features on this page don't show.

Agents can make mistakes. Check what they write before you rely on it or share it.

Chat with an agent

  1. Click Agents (the sparkle icon) in the app rail on the right edge of the window. The Agents panel opens.
  2. If your server has several agents, pick one with the agent menu in the panel's header ("Choose an Agent"). Your choice is kept, and also used by the other AI features.
  3. Type your question in "Ask Agents anything..." and press Enter. You can also drop a file onto the panel to give it to the agent.

The agent answers in the panel. While it writes, you can click Stop Generating; once it is done, Regenerate asks for another answer. When the agent used tools or searched the web, its answer says so, for example "Used 3 tools" or "Searched the web for …".

Each question you ask from the panel starts a conversation; reply in it to go on. Click New chat to start another one, and View chat history to find your earlier conversations. A small ring shows how much of the agent's memory (its context window) the conversation uses.

The panel is a direct message with the agent: you'll also find it among your direct messages, and you can write to the agent there. Each new message starts a new conversation, answered in a thread.

Ask about the channel you are reading

When you open the Agents panel while reading a channel, it offers to talk "About #channel": click Summarize unreads, Summarize the last 7 days or Summarize the last 14 days, or type a question in "Ask about …".

Custom prompts

If you often ask the same thing, save it as a custom prompt with Manage prompts, in the AI menu of the message box. Pinned prompts appear as buttons in the Agents panel.

Ask an agent in a channel

To ask an agent in front of everyone, mention it like a person: type @ and the agent's name, then your question. The agent answers in a thread under your message, which everyone in the channel can read. It reads that thread, not the whole channel.

To go on with the agent in a thread, mention it again in your reply. If you forget, Antimatter reminds you, only to you, with a link to loop the agent in.

An agent doesn't answer if you aren't allowed to use it, or if it isn't allowed in that channel. Some channels have an agent that replies automatically: their admins set this up in the channel's settings.

AI actions on a message

  1. Point at a message and click More actions (⋯).
  2. Point at AI actions.
  3. Choose one:
    • Summarize Thread: a summary of the thread the message is in.
    • Find action items: the tasks and who should do them.
    • Find open questions: the questions still waiting for an answer.
    • React for me: the agent picks a fitting emoji and adds it as your reaction.

The answer appears in the Agents panel, in your private conversation with the agent: nobody else sees it. The same actions are in the thread panel's Thread actions menu.

Catch up on a channel

When you open a channel with unread messages, the "Unread" line that marks where they start has an Ask AI button. Click it, pick the agent under "Generate With:", then choose Summarize new messages, Find action items or Find open questions. The answer shows in the Agents panel, visible only to you.

Channel summaries cover 14 days at most.

Rewrite your message before sending it

  1. Write your message.
  2. Click Show formatting (Aa) if the formatting bar is hidden, then click AI at its left end.
  3. Under Rewrite with AI, choose Improve writing, Fix spelling & grammar, Make it shorter, Make it more formal or Translate…. For a translation, type the language, such as "French", and press Enter.

The message box shows "The AI is rewriting your message…", then replaces your text with the new one, marked Rewritten by AI. Read it, change it if you like, and send it. Click Undo to get your own text back. For a reply in a thread, the agent also reads the start of the thread and its last replies, so the rewrite fits the conversation.

The same menu lists your custom prompts, under "GENERATE WITH:", where you can also pick the agent.

Recaps

A recap is a summary of several channels, prepared for you by an agent: once, or on a schedule, such as every weekday morning. Recaps are available when your administrator turned them on. You then find Recaps in the list of your direct messages.

Create a recap

  1. Open Recaps and click Add a recap.
  2. Give your recap a name.
  3. Choose Recap selected channels or Recap all my unreads.
  4. Turn on Run once to get a recap right away, without a schedule.
  5. For selected channels, pick the channels to include.
  6. For a single recap, check the list of channels and click Start recap. For a scheduled one, choose the days and the time it runs, the Time period to cover (Last 24 hours, Last 7 days or Since last read), add instructions for the agent if you like, and click Create schedule.

A recap takes a moment to prepare. It then appears under Unread, with a card for each channel: its highlights and action items. From a recap's menu you can open a channel, mark it as read, regenerate the recap or delete it. Your scheduled recaps are under Scheduled, where you can pause them. Your administrator limits how many recaps you can run each day.

Meeting summaries

When a call was transcribed (see Transcription and live captions), the transcript's message in the call's thread can offer Create meeting summary. The agent writes the summary in your private conversation with it, and can offer to post it in the call's thread for everyone.

Search with an agent

If your administrator turned on AI search, the search box in the conversation header offers an Agents option in its suggestions. Choose it, then ask a question in your own words: the agent looks for the answer in the messages of the channels you are a member of, and answers in the Agents panel.

Tools and web search

Agents can use tools that your administrator connects, for example to look something up in another system, or to search the web. The panel's Tools menu lists the tool providers: you can turn each one off or on for yourself, and Connect to those that need you to sign in.

Some tools ask for your approval before they run: the agent shows a card with what it wants to do, and you click Accept or Reject. In a channel, there is a second step after the tool ran: choose Share to let the agent use the result in its answer, which everyone in the channel will see, or Keep private.

Agents run tools with your permissions: they can't reach anything you can't.

What agents see

  • An agent only reads what you could read yourself. Summaries, recaps and searches check that you may read each channel, and AI search only covers channels you are a member of.
  • When you mention an agent in a thread, it reads that thread. A summary reads the thread, or the channel over the period you chose. A rewrite reads your draft, and in a thread also the start of the thread and its last replies.
  • With each request, the agent also receives some context: the server's name, who is asking, and the channel and community.
  • What the agent reads is sent to the AI service your administrator chose for it. That service may be outside your organisation: ask your administrator if you need to know where your data goes.
  • Your conversations with an agent are stored on your Antimatter server, like other messages.
  • Answers to AI actions, channel summaries and searches are posted in your private conversation with the agent: only you see them. Answers to mentions in a channel are visible to everyone in it.

If you don't see the agents

If the Agents panel says "No agents yet", your administrator hasn't set one up that you can use. If your server lets people create their own agents, the panel offers Create an agent, using an AI service your administrator configured. Without a usable agent, the AI button of the message box, the AI actions menu and Ask AI don't show.
