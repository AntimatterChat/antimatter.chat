Here is how to open each one and what it is for. ---

Find and open the apps

Your administrator chooses which apps your server has, so you may not see all of those on this page. Most apps have an icon in the app rail, the narrow column on the right edge of the window, under your picture. Point at an icon to see the app's name, and click it to open the app in a panel beside the conversation. Click it again to close the panel. Drag the edge of the panel to make it wider; Restore default width puts it back.

Apps also appear in other places:

  • The + menu of the message box (Add a file and more), for example Create a poll.
  • The tabs of the emoji picker: GIFs, Stickers and Memes.
  • The Message actions part of a message's More actions menu, for example Add to todo or Run playbook.
  • Slash commands, such as /poll or /todo: type / to see the list.
  • Full pages: Kanban and Playbooks are listed under Products in the About Antimatter menu, at the bottom of the app rail. Their panel also has an Open as a full page button.
  • The settings of some apps are under Plugins in your settings.

On a phone, the app rail is in a drawer on the right: swipe left, or choose More › Members and apps in the conversation's header.

Notes

Notes are documents that several people edit at the same time, beside the chat. You see the others' cursors and names as they type, and everything is saved as you go.

Write a note

  1. Click Notes in the app rail.
  2. Choose a tab: This channel for a note every member of the channel can read and edit, or Mine for a personal note only you can see.
  3. Click + (New note in this channel or New personal note). An "Untitled note" opens.
  4. Type its title, then write. The Formatting bar has bold, italic, strikethrough, headings, lists and checklists; links, quotes and code work too. Type @ to mention a member of the channel (mentions in notes don't notify anyone).
  5. Click All notes to go back to the list.

When a note in a channel gets its title, Antimatter posts "Shared a note" in the channel, with an Open note button.

More with a note

Star a note to find it in the Favorites tab. The note's ⋯ menu offers Share in the channel (posts a card linking to it), Share in ~channel (makes a personal note a channel note, for good), Version history (see and Restore this version), Export as Markdown and Delete. Only the note's creator and the channel's admins can delete a channel note.

Whiteboard

A whiteboard is a canvas you draw on together: shapes, arrows, text and freehand drawing, with everyone's pointers shown live.

  1. Click Whiteboard in the app rail.
  2. As with notes, choose This channel or Mine, and click + (New board in this channel or New personal board).
  3. Draw with the tools: Select and move (V), Move around (H), Rectangle (R), Ellipse (O), Arrow (A), Draw (P), Text (T) and Eraser (E). Choose colours, line styles and fonts in the toolbar, and shapes in the Library. Undo (Ctrl+Z) undoes your last change.

The Board actions menu has Full screen, Show the whole board, downloads as .excalidraw, PNG or SVG, Version history, Import an .excalidraw file… (it replaces the board for everyone), sharing in the channel, and Delete. Whiteboards stay on your Antimatter server.

Kanban

Kanban boards organise work as cards in columns, such as "To do", "Doing" and "Done". A board can also be shown as a table, a gallery or a calendar.

Open the boards of a channel

Click Kanban in the app rail. The panel lists the boards linked to the channel; click one to open it. If none is linked yet, click Link boards to …, then Link next to a board, or Create a board. The members of the channel become editors of a linked board.

Work with a board

  • To see all your boards, open Kanban as a full page: Open as a full page in the panel, or About Antimatter › Products › Kanban.
  • To create a board, click + Add board, then start from a template (meeting agenda, project tasks, roadmap, sprint planner and others) or Create an empty board.
  • Click + New in a column to add a card. Open a card to set its properties, add text, checkboxes and images, attach files, and comment. Mentioning someone in a card notifies them.
  • Drag cards between columns. Use Group by, Filter, Sort and Search cards to find your way, and Add view for a table, gallery or calendar view.
  • Use Share on a board to choose who can see or edit it.

Calendar

The Calendar app shows your own calendar beside the chat, sends you reminders, and lets you meet in Antimatter. It works with calendar servers that use CalDAV, such as Nextcloud, Fastmail or Radicale.

Connect your calendar

  1. Click Calendar in the app rail.
  2. In Connect your calendar, fill in the CalDAV server address (often just your provider's domain), your Username and your Password. If your provider offers app passwords, use one.
  3. Choose when to Remind me of events without alarms, then click Connect.

Your password is stored encrypted on the Antimatter server. To change these details later, or disconnect, use Account settings in the panel.

Use it

  • Switch between Agenda and Week, move with Previous, Today and Next, and click a calendar's chip to show or hide it.
  • Click New event, fill in the Title, Starts and Ends, and optionally Repeat, Reminder, Location and Description, then Save. Click an event to change or delete it.
  • To meet in Antimatter, set Meet in Antimatter to a channel or voice channel, or to a call, and pick the Channel. When the time comes, the event shows Start call, Join call or Join.
  • Reminders arrive as direct messages from the Calendar bot, with a link to join the call when there is one.

The app shows your own calendars; it doesn't send invitations to other people.

Mail

The Mail app shows your mailbox beside the chat, so you can read, answer and share emails without leaving Antimatter.

Connect your mailbox

  1. Click Mail in the app rail.
  2. If your administrator set it up, click Sign in with Google or Sign in with Microsoft. Otherwise, or for another provider, fill in your Email address and Your name, then the Incoming mail (IMAP) and Outgoing mail (SMTP) servers, with their security, port, username and password. Your provider's help pages give these details.
  3. Click Connect.

Use it

  • Pick a Folder, search with Search this folder, and click a message to read it. Images from the internet are hidden until you click Show images.
  • Answer with Reply, Reply all or Forward, or write with New message. Messages are plain text, and you can't attach new files.
  • Click Share to a channel to post an email's sender, subject and an excerpt in a channel, with a comment.

Polls

A poll asks a question in a conversation, and everyone votes with one click. Results update live.

  1. Click the + in the message box and choose Create a poll, or type /poll.
  2. Type the Question and at least two answers; Add answer adds more, up to ten.
  3. Turn on Allow multiple answers if people may pick several, and Anonymous voting if nobody should see who voted for what.
  4. Choose a Duration, from 1 hour to 1 week, or No end.
  5. Click Post poll.

To vote, click an answer; click it again to take your vote back. The poll ends at the end of its duration, or when its creator or a channel admin clicks Close poll; it then shows the Final results.

You can also write a whole poll in one line: /poll "Lunch?" "Pizza" "Sushi" --multi --duration 1h. Type /poll help for all the options.

Todo

Todo keeps to-do lists: your own, shared lists for a channel, and tasks you send to other people.

  • Click Todo in the app rail. In the This channel tab, add a task to the channel's shared list, and use Assign to… to give it to a member. Star the list to follow it; the lists you follow are gathered in the Following tab.
  • The Mine tab holds your own tasks, those assigned to you in channels, and those sent to you or by you. Pick someone with Send to… to send them a task: they can Accept, Decline or Mark as done it.
  • To turn a message into a task, choose Message actions › Add to todo in its menu.
  • Click Mark as done when a task is finished.

You can also use /todo add …, /todo list and /todo send @someone … for your personal tasks.

GIFs, stickers and memes

Click Emoji (the smiley face) in the message box: when these apps are set up, the picker has GIFs, Stickers and Memes tabs next to Emoji.

  • GIFs: search, or browse Favorites, Recently used, Trending and the categories. Click a GIF to send it at once. Click its star to add it to your favourites. You can also type /gif and a few words.
  • Stickers: search or browse the sticker packs, and click a sticker to send it. /sticker works too.
  • Memes: pick a template, fill in its texts in Make a meme, watch the preview, and click Post meme. You can also type /meme, or /meme help to see how to write one in a line.

Depending on your server's settings, you may be able to add your own GIFs, stickers or meme templates with the + next to the search box.

Playbooks

A playbook is a reusable checklist for work that follows the same steps each time: an incident, a release, welcoming a new colleague. Each time you use it, you start a run, with its own tasks, owners, status updates and, at the end, a retrospective.

  • Click Playbooks in the app rail to see the runs of the current channel, start one with Run a playbook, or make a quick New checklist.
  • To create or edit playbooks, open the full page: About Antimatter › Products › Playbooks. Start from a template, such as product release, incident resolution or employee onboarding, or from a blank playbook.
  • To start a run, click Run on a playbook, give the run a name and summary, choose to create a channel for it or link it to an existing one, and click Start run. You can also choose Message actions › Run playbook on a message.
  • During a run, tick off tasks, assign them and give them due dates, and click Post update to tell everyone where things stand. The owner clicks Finish at the end.

Type /playbook to see the commands, such as /playbook run or /playbook update.

GitHub, GitLab and Jira

These apps connect Antimatter to the tools developers use. Your administrator must set them up first. Then:

  1. Connect your account by typing /github connect, /gitlab connect or /jira connect, then follow the link and sign in on that site. For GitLab, do this in a web browser, not in the desktop app.
  2. You then get direct messages when someone mentions you, asks for your review or assigns you something.
  3. To have a channel follow a repository or a project, use /github subscriptions add owner/repo, /gitlab subscriptions add group/project, or /jira subscribe.
  4. To create or attach an issue from a message, choose Create GitHub Issue, Create GitLab Issue, Create Jira Issue or the matching Attach to … item in the message's Message actions.

Type /github help, /gitlab help or /jira help for all their commands.

Apps on the mobile app

The mobile app can create and vote in polls, send GIFs and stickers, and answer tasks sent to you. Notes and whiteboards open in a browser window inside the app. Kanban, Calendar, Mail and memes are not in the mobile app yet: use the web or desktop app for them.
