# Project Aurora: final report

Adaptive circadian lighting pilot, Maplewood care home, January to April 2026.

## Setup
- 40 Lumen Arcs in 4 corridors and the day room
- On-device scheduler, night rounds at 10% amber
- Read-only dashboard: light levels per corridor, no personal data

## Results (week 12)
- Night-time call-button presses: −18% (one home, 14 weeks: promising, not conclusive)
- Staff rating of night rounds: 4.6 / 5
- Scheduler faults during Wi-Fi outages: 0

## Next
The scheduler ships to everyone as circadian schedules in Lumen OS 2.5. Maplewood keeps the 40 Arcs.
