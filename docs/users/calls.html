Calls and voice channels

Talk with your team: call someone from a conversation, or drop into a voice channel that is always open. This page covers calls, voice channels and everything you can do during a call.

Before you start

Calls are an app that your administrator installs and turns on. If you see no phone button in the header of your conversations, calls are not available on your server, or not in that channel.

The first time you join a call, your browser or the desktop app asks to use your microphone. Allow it, or nobody will hear you. To choose which microphone and speakers Antimatter uses, see Choose your microphone, speakers and camera.

Start a call

In a channel or a conversation

  1. Open the channel, direct message or group message.
  2. Click Start a call (the phone at the top right of the conversation).

The call starts and you are in it. A card in the conversation tells the others that a call started, with a Join button. You stay on the conversation's messages: the call shows in the dock at the bottom of the sidebar (see The call dock).

With one person

Click someone's name or picture to open their profile card, then click Call. Antimatter opens your direct message with them and starts a call there. You can also right-click a person and choose Call.

Ringing

When you start a call in a direct or group message, the others hear a ring if your administrator turned ringing on. A card appears in the top right corner of their screen, wherever they are in Antimatter: "is calling you", or "… is calling the group". They can click Join, Decline, or Mute the ring to stop the sound while keeping the card. If nobody joins within about 30 seconds, the call stops, and its card in the conversation says No answer, Declined or Canceled.

You are not rung while you are in another call, while your status is Do not disturb or out of office, in a conversation you muted, or by someone you ignore or block.

To choose the ring, or turn it off, go to Settings › Notifications, under Desktop and browser: turn Incoming call ring on or off, and pick a sound in Ring.

Join or leave a call

When a call is running in a channel or conversation you are not in, you can join it in several ways:

  • Click Join on the call's card in the conversation.
  • Click Join call in the banner under the conversation's header ("… started a call").
  • Click the green Join button in the header, which shows how many people are in the call.

In the sidebar, a green phone after a channel's name, or an In call badge on a conversation, shows that a call is running there. Conversations with a call in progress move to the top of your direct messages while the call lasts.

You can be in one call at a time: joining another call leaves the one you are in.

To leave a call, click Leave the call (the red phone in the header), Disconnect in the call dock or in the call controls, or press Ctrl+Shift+L (⌘+Shift+L on a Mac).

Voice channels

A voice channel is a call room that is always open: no one has to start a call. People come and go as they like, and everyone in the community sees who is in it, listed under the channel in the sidebar. Voice channels have a speaker icon instead of a #.

Join a voice channel

  1. Click the voice channel in the sidebar. You are connected at once ("Connected to …"), and you stay on the conversation you were reading.
  2. To see the people in the room, click the voice channel again, or click Show the voice channel in the call dock.

On some servers, clicking a voice channel opens the room without joining it: click Join voice to join. Going to another voice channel moves you there.

Its text chat

Each voice channel also has messages, for links and notes during the conversation. Click Open text chat next to the channel in the sidebar, or Text chat in its header, to open them in the panel on the right. You don't have to be in the call to read or write there.

Deafen

In a voice channel, Deafen stops all sound from the call, and mutes you too. Click it again, or unmute, to hear the others again.

Create a voice channel

If you can create channels, choose Create channel in the community menu, pick the Voice channel type, give it a name, and create it. See Create a channel.

The call dock

While you are in a call or a voice channel, a dock at the bottom of the sidebar shows Voice connected or Call connected, and where the call is. Click that name to go to its conversation. The dock has these buttons:

  • Mute or Unmute your microphone.
  • Deafen (in voice channels).
  • Camera, where video is allowed.
  • Share screen, or Stop sharing.
  • Show the call (or Show the voice channel), which opens the call in the main area.
  • Disconnect, to leave.

When someone shares their screen, a small preview appears in the dock. Click it to see the call.

Show the call

A call can show in three places:

  • In the main area, in place of the messages: click Show the call in the conversation's header or in the dock. Click Show the messages to go back to the conversation. In a call shown this way, Call chat opens the call's thread beside it.
  • In a small floating window that you can drag around: press Ctrl+Shift+P (⌘+Shift+P on a Mac). Click Back to the sidebar to close it; you stay in the call.
  • In a window of its own: click Open in a new window in the call controls. If your browser blocks pop-up windows, the call opens in the floating window instead. Closing the window brings the call back into Antimatter.

Each person has a tile. Click Pin to the stage on a tile, or double-click it, to keep that person large; click Unpin to undo. On a camera or a shared screen, Picture in picture keeps it visible over your other windows, where your browser supports it, and Full screen shows a shared screen on your whole screen.

During a call

The controls under the call let you:

ButtonWhat it does
Mute / UnmuteTurn your microphone off or on. Shortcut: Ctrl+Shift+Space (⌘+Shift+Space on a Mac).
DeafenStop hearing the call, in voice channels.
CameraTurn your camera on or off, where video is allowed.
Share screen / Stop sharingShare a screen, a window or a browser tab. Shortcut: Ctrl+Shift+E.
Raise hand / Lower handAsk to speak. Shortcut: Ctrl+Shift+Y.
ReactSend a quick reaction that floats over the call for a few seconds.
Show live captionsShow what people say as text, while the call is transcribed.
Call chatOpen the call's thread, for calls started in a conversation.
Open in a new windowMove the call to a window of its own.
DisconnectLeave the call.

On a Mac, Ctrl is ⌘ in these shortcuts. You can change them in Settings › Keyboard.

Video

Cameras work in direct messages between two people and in voice channels, when your administrator turned video on. Elsewhere, the Camera button says "Cameras are turned off in this call". Click the small arrow on the Camera button (Camera options) to turn on Blur my background and choose its Amount: Light, Medium or Strong.

Screen sharing

  1. Click Share screen. To choose what and how, click its small arrow (Screen share options) first.
  2. In Share your screen, choose A whole screen, A window or A browser tab (some browsers only offer Choose a screen or a window…). You can also pick a Resolution, from 480p to Source, and a Frame rate of 15, 30 or 60 fps. Antimatter remembers your choice.
  3. Pick the screen or window in the dialog that follows.

One person can share at a time. In the desktop app, Antimatter shows its own picker, Choose what to share, with your Screens and Windows; on Windows and Linux you can tick Also share the computer's sound. On a Mac, the system asks you once to allow screen recording for the app.

Raise your hand

Click Raise hand to show that you would like to speak. A hand appears on your tile with your place in the queue, such as "Hand raised, 1st in line". Click Lower hand when you are done.

Change someone's volume

Right-click a person's tile, or their name under the voice channel in the sidebar, and move the Volume slider. Antimatter remembers it for that person, in this browser.

Push to talk

If you prefer to stay muted and talk only while you hold a key, go to Settings › Keyboard, click Change next to Push to talk in a call (hold), and press the key you want. During a call, hold that key to talk. It works while the Antimatter window is in front.

Muting and other moderator actions

The person who started the call, and the channel's and community's admins, can moderate it. In voice channels, the community's moderators can too. Right-click a person to open their menu; under "In …" a moderator finds:

  • Mute: mutes someone who is talking. They can unmute themselves.
  • Ask to unmute: asks someone who is muted to speak. They see "… asks you to unmute", with Unmute and Not now.
  • Mute for this call: keeps someone muted until you allow it, even if they leave and come back. Allow to unmute lifts it.
  • Lower hand and Stop screen share.
  • Remove from call, or Kick from voice channel.

If a moderator muted you for the call, your microphone button reads "A moderator muted you", and you can unmute once they allow it.

Everyone also finds Profile, Message, Call, Copy username and Volume in that menu.

Transcription and live captions

When your administrator set up transcription, the person who started a call can click Transcribe the call in the controls. Everyone then sees a Transcribing badge: what everyone says is turned into text. When the host clicks Stop transcribing, or the call ends, the transcript is posted as a text file in the call's thread ("Here's the call transcription"), or in the channel for a voice channel.

While a call is transcribed, and if your administrator turned live captions on, click Show live captions to see the last lines spoken, with the speakers' names, at the bottom of the call. Click Hide live captions to hide them. If AI agents are set up, a summary of the meeting can be written from the transcript: see AI agents.

Choose your microphone, speakers and camera

  1. Open Settings (click your picture at the top right, then Profile & settings).
  2. Under Plugins, choose Calls.
  3. In Audio devices, click Edit and choose your Microphone input and Speaker output, then Save.
  4. When video is available, Video devices lets you choose the Camera input, mirror your video and set the background blur.
  5. Screen sharing settings sets whether the sound of what you share is included (Share sound with screen).

During a call, Camera and microphone settings… in the camera options opens the same page. If Antimatter can't use your microphone, a message tells you, for example "Allow access to your microphone to talk in the call": allow it in your browser's or system's settings.

Calls on the mobile app

The mobile app can start and join calls and voice channels, mute, deafen, raise your hand and show live captions. It doesn't have cameras, push to talk or per-person volume. See The desktop and mobile apps.
